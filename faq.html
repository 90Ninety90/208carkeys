Frequently Asked Questions

Mobile locksmith service for homes, cars, and businesses across Boise and the Treasure Valley. Call 208-365-5625 for help.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. We provide 24/7 emergency locksmith service and same-day appointments whenever possible.

Can you help if I’m locked out of my car?

Yes. We use non-destructive entry methods whenever possible and can help with most makes and models.

Do you program key fobs and transponder keys?

Yes. We cut and program transponder keys, smart keys, proximity fobs, and remote head keys on-site.

What information should I have ready?

Please have your year, make, model, and location ready. Proof of ownership is required for key creation.

How quickly can you arrive?

Arrival times vary by location and current call volume, but we aim for the fastest possible response across the Treasure Valley.

Do you service European vehicles?

Yes. We regularly work on BMW, Mercedes, Audi, Volkswagen, Porsche, Jaguar, Land Rover, and more.
